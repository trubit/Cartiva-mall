# TrusonShopp Platform — Workflows & Automation Engine

## Overview

The Workflow Engine (Phase 25) enables low-code business process automation across e-commerce operations.

---

## Capabilities

- **Triggers**: Event-driven (e.g. `order.created`, `user.registered`), scheduled crons, or API webhooks.
- **Actions**: Send Email, Update Stock, Trigger Webhook, Apply Promotion, Notify Seller, Rebalance Logistics.
- **Approvals**: Multi-step human approval gates for sensitive operational actions.
- **Audit**: Every step execution is logged in `WorkflowExecution` and `WorkflowAudit`.

---

## Integration with God-Mode AI (Phase 29)

The God-Mode AI Economic Brain can mutate workflow rules dynamically to stabilize the marketplace under stress.
For example, under `STRESSED` economic conditions, God-Mode can automatically enable additional approval gates for high-value seller payouts.
